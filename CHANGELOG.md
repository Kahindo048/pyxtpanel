# Changelog

All notable changes are recorded here. The project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/); versions 0.x may change the API, which is frozen only at 1.0.

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Repository, tooling configuration and package skeleton (architecture validated 2026-11-07).
- Panel declaration (`xtset`), calendar time axis (integers with `delta`, dates, Stata `%t` formats), selection, gaps, `tsfill`, balance classes, `.dta` read/write with `xtset` characteristics.
- Time-series operators `L F D S` and Stata notation (`"L2D.x"`, `"L(1/3).x"`) on the calendar: `PanelData.operate`, `lag`, `lead`, `diff`, `seasonal`; validated against Stata on 12 panels.
- Transformations: `within` (unit, period, two-way with a convergence report), `between`, `quasi_demean` and `theta_re`, forward orthogonal deviations `fod`, group means and counts, `rolling`, `cumulative`, `growth`; generic `PanelData.transform(name, ...)` over a registry of operators. Validated against `xtdata`, `regress ... i.id i.t` and `xtreg, re`.
- Array contract for the estimation packages: `PanelData.arrays(columns, where=...)` returns a frozen `PanelArrays` (`pyxtpanel.contract`, `CONTRACT_VERSION = 1`) with `offsets`, `time_index`, `lag_index`, `reindex_back`, `as_cube`.
- `PanelBase.with_frame` to reuse the sorted index when only the values change.
- Description of panels: `PanelData.xtdescribe` (participation patterns, distribution of T_i), `xtsum` (overall, between, within; `by=`), `xttab`, `xttrans` (gaps bridged as in Stata or `gaps="strict"`, `bins=`), `xtcorr` (overall, within, between with pair counts), `autocorr`, `attrition` (entries, exits, Kaplan-Meier survival of units), `missing_report` and `complete_cases`. Validated against Stata 17 (`xtdescribe`, `xtsum`, `xttab`, `xttrans`, `xtdata` + `correlate`).
- Public API: `as_panel`, `PanelLike`, top-level functions for every description and `operate`/`transform`; `Result.to_markdown` and `to_latex` for all results; HTML display of panels; table of kept Stata return codes (`STATA_CODES`).
- Tooling: `tools/stata_map.py` (generates `docs/stata_map.md`), `tools/snapshot_api.py` (API snapshot test), `tools/nouveau.py` (template for a new operator or description), `tools/avancement.py` (plan progress).
- Figures (extra `plot`, needs matplotlib): `PanelData.plot(kind, ...)` with `lines` (like `xtline`), `scatter` (between and within), `distribution`, `participation` and `transition`; same chart style as `pyxtdpthresh`, English and French (decimal comma), PNG/PDF/SVG export, `python -m tools.galerie`.
- Exploration tools (block K, no Stata equivalent): `PanelData.gap_types` (late entry, early exit, internal gap, re-entry), `unbalancedness` (Ahrens-Pincus gamma and nu as `plm::punbalancedness`), `balance_cost` (units and observations kept by each minimum T_i and each window, largest balanced block), `usable_equations` (equations left for a dynamic-panel estimator with first differences or orthogonal deviations; same counts as `pyxtabond2`). Registry `TOOLS` with the validation method of each tool.
- Exploration tools, continued (block K): `variation`, `residual_variation` (what within, first differences and FOD leave), `spells`, `event_time` (indicator or event period), `within_outliers` (modified z-score, flagged not removed) and `level_shifts`. Compared with Stata (`egen`, `bysort`, calendar lags) on 8 simulated panels; `egen median()` is imprecise in float, noted in `docs/stata_map.md`.
- Conversions and duplicates (block K): `PanelData.to_wide` (as `reshape wide`, checked against Stata), `to_long` (as `reshape long`, on a DataFrame), `PanelData.to_array3d` (variable x period x unit with the mask of rows) and `resolve_duplicates` (first, last, mean, sum, min, max, median, with a report of what was merged).
- Panel audit (block K): `xt.audit(panel_or_frame)` and `PanelData.audit()` run the registered checks (structure, balance, missing values, variation, outliers, equations with `dependent=`) and return an `AuditReport` of alerts with advice in French and English, exportable to text, Markdown, HTML (Jupyter), LaTeX. Thresholds are adjustable; raw data with repeated pairs get a critical alert. `python -m tools.nouveau controle <name>` creates a new check. State of the art completed (ETAT_DES_LIEUX section 7): `xtbalfront`, `tsspell` and `tsreport` are the Stata neighbours of `balance_cost`, `spells` and `gap_types`.
- Figures, continued: `plot("states")` (units by periods coloured by state, like `panelview`, missing cells distinct), `plot("attrition")` (units observed, entries and exits, Kaplan-Meier survival) and `plot("balance_cost")` (share kept by minimum length and by window, largest block marked); gallery now has 10 figures.
- Statistical tests, infrastructure (block L.1): `TestResult` (statistic, p-value, degrees of freedom, hypotheses in French and English, `to_frame`, `summary`, HTML), registry `TESTS` with `TestSpec` (`register_test`, `list_tests`, `run_test`), normalised inputs (`panel_variables` refuses an unbalanced panel or a gap and names the first offending unit; `as_array` for category B). A contract test requires, for every registered test, a validation case run in Stata 17 and frozen in `tests/golden/` (no exception). `python -m tools.nouveau test NAME --famille F --categorie A|B` creates the module, its unit test and its Stata case. Families added for residual and specification tests (plan L.8): `heteroskedasticity`, `effects`, `normality`, `specification`, `heterogeneity`.
- `hansen` and `sargan` (category B): J and Sargan tests of over-identifying restrictions from bare arrays (moments, weight matrix, number of parameters, scale); `moment_covariance`; remarks for a model that is not over-identified or has more instruments than groups. Validated against Stata 17 `ivregress` + `estat overid` and `xtabond2` (relative difference below 2e-10). `TestResult.notes` holds translatable `Note` objects.
- `hausman` (consistent versus efficient estimator, Stata's generalised inverse and degrees of freedom, negative statistic reported as Stata does) and `wald` (joint chi-square or F test of `R b = r`), both from bare arrays. Validated against Stata 17 `hausman` and `test` on coefficients and covariances exported from `xtreg`, `regress` and `ivregress`.
- `ar_test` (category B): Arellano-Bond m_k test of serial correlation, robust form, from `Equations` (residuals, regressors, instruments, calendar positions) with the weight matrix and covariance of the estimator; `min_pairs` for estimators that require a minimum. Validated against Stata 17 `xtabond2` (`e(ar1)`, `e(ar2)`, one-step and two-step, balanced, gaps, entry and exit) and `xtabond` + `estat abond`. `wald_groupwise` (`xttest3`), `lm_random_effects` (`xttest0`), `f_effects` (F of `xtreg, fe`), `skewness_kurtosis` (`sktest`, with Royston's adjustment) and `jarque_bera`, all from bare arrays and validated against Stata 17. `breusch_pagan` (variants `normal`, `iid`, `fstat`) and `white` heteroskedasticity tests from residuals and regressors, validated against `estat hettest` and `estat imtest, white`; auxiliary least squares in `diagnostics/_shared/ols.py`. `diff_hansen` (C statistic of the exogeneity of an instrument subset, from the two Hansen statistics; validated against the difference-in-Hansen tests of `xtabond2`). The category B tests are importable from `pyxtpanel.diagnostics` (`hansen`, `sargan`, `hausman`, `wald`, `ar_test`, `moment_covariance`).
- First category A tests (they read a declared panel and variable names): `pesaran_cd` (Pesaran CD test of cross-sectional dependence on a variable or on residuals added to the frame; each pair of units uses its common periods with weight `sqrt(T_ij)`, so it accepts unbalanced panels and refuses, naming the two units, a pair with fewer than 3 common periods) and `wooldridge` (Wooldridge test of first-order autocorrelation from first differences, with a covariance clustered by unit; differences and lags follow the calendar, so holes, entry and exit are accepted as in Stata). Validated against Stata 17: `xtcsd, pesaran` after `xtreg, fe` (balanced, entry/exit/holes, holes), `xtcd2, noest` (balanced) and `xtserial` (AR(1) errors, independent errors, holes, entry and exit). Finding: on an unbalanced panel `xtcd2` does not use the formula of `xtcsd` (it fills the panel and correlates unit means with all non-missing values), so its CD differs; this package follows Pesaran (2015) and `xtcsd`.
- `harris_tzavalis` (category A, unit roots): Harris-Tzavalis test of a common AR parameter for large N and fixed T, with `deterministics` (`none`, `constant`, `trend`), `demean` and `small_sample_t`; needs a strongly balanced panel without gaps. Validated against Stata 17 `xtunitroot ht` (6 cases, relative difference below 1e-9).
- `fisher` (category A, unit roots): Fisher-type test from the p-values of one augmented Dickey-Fuller test per unit (`method`: inverse chi-squared `P`, inverse normal `Z`, logit `L`, modified `Pm`), fixed `lags`, `deterministics` (`constant`, `trend`, `drift`), `demean`; unbalanced panels and holes accepted; per-unit table in `details`. Validated against Stata 17 `xtunitroot fisher, dfuller` (6 cases, relative difference below 1e-9). Embedded numerical tables (`diagnostics/_shared/tables.py`): CSV files with a manifest (source, date, verification, SHA-256) that is checked on every load; first table: MacKinnon (1994) p-values of the Dickey-Fuller statistic, checked against statsmodels and Stata.
- `levin_lin_chu` (category A, unit roots): Levin-Lin-Chu test with the bias-adjusted `t*` (models `none`, `constant`, `trend`; fixed lags or lags chosen by AIC, BIC or HQIC; Bartlett, Parzen and quadratic-spectral kernels; fixed, `llc` or `nwest` bandwidth; `demean`; `interpolate`); needs a strongly balanced panel without gaps. Adjustment table `llc_adjustments.csv` (Levin, Lin and Chu 2002, Table 2). Validated against Stata 17 `xtunitroot llc` (12 cases, relative difference below 1e-9). `python -m tools.manifest_tables --write|--check` maintains the checksums of the embedded tables.
- `im_pesaran_shin` (category A, unit roots): Im-Pesaran-Shin test for heterogeneous panels. Without `lags`: `t-bar` (with the exact critical values of the paper for a balanced panel), `t-tilde-bar` and `Z-t-tilde-bar` (the returned statistic, no p-value when a unit is too short); with `lags` (fixed, or the maximum with `lag_selection` aic/bic/hqic): `W-t-bar`. `deterministics` constant or trend, `demean`, `interpolate`; unbalanced panels without gaps accepted. Tables `ips_table1/2/3.csv`. Validated against Stata 17 `xtunitroot ips` (12 cases, relative difference below 1e-9). Note: Stata's Table 3 has -2.204 for (trend, 8 lags, T = 60), apparently a transposition of -2.024; kept to equal Stata and flagged in the manifest.
- Calling the tests on a panel: `unit_root`, `cross_section_dependence` and `serial_correlation` (top-level functions and `PanelData` methods), the generic `PanelData.test(name, ...)`, Stata subcommand names as aliases (`ht`, `llc`, `ips`, `cd`, `xtserial`; `run_test` accepts them too). No default test; an option the test does not have, a missing argument, a test of another family, a test on arrays or an undeclared frame is refused before running, in French or English, with the arguments the test accepts. A contract test checks that the balance each test declares is the balance it enforces.
- Cross-section dependence, continued: `lm_cross_section` (Breusch-Pagan LM, alias `xttest2`; validated against an independent Mata implementation because `xttest2` is not installed), `friedman` and `frees` (Spearman correlations over the periods common to all units, as `xtcsd` does; table `frees_q.csv` of Frees' Q critical values; validated against `xtcsd, friedman` and `xtcsd, frees`), and the descriptive `cross_correlation` (matrix of correlations between units over each pair's common periods, with the average correlation and absolute correlation; pairs too short are `NaN`). Shared brick `diagnostics/_shared/pairwise.py`. `pesaran_cd` now uses it; its results are unchanged.
- Normality tests, continued (category B, bare residuals): `shapiro_wilk` (`swilk`, alias `swilk`; Royston's coefficients and normal approximation, exact p-value for n = 3, average ranks for ties or `ties=False`) and `shapiro_francia` (`sfrancia`, alias `sfrancia`; Royston's log transformation or `transform="boxcox"`). Both return W, V and z in `details`. Validated against Stata 17 on every branch of the approximation (n = 3, 4, 5, 7, 11, 12, 15, 900, 1500, 3521, with and without ties); `swilk` at n = 3 with W at its lower bound returns a p-value of 0 where Stata prints a slightly negative one (noted in the result). `swilk`'s `lnnormal` option is not taken.
- Specification tests (category B, bare arrays): `reset` (Ramsey RESET, alias `ovtest`; powers 2-4 of the fitted values or, with `rhs=True`, of each non-binary regressor, rescaled to [0, 1] as Stata does; collinear powers are left out and counted in a remark) and `vif` (variance inflation factors, centered or `uncentered`, a descriptive `VarianceInflation` result like `cross_correlation`, not a test). Validated against Stata 17 `estat ovtest` (to 3e-6, `estat ovtest` builds the powers as single-precision variables; also against the same test computed in double precision in Stata, to 2e-13) and `estat vif` (to 1e-9 with `set type double`, Stata keeps the factors in a float variable). A regressor that is constant or an exact linear combination of the others is refused by `vif` where Stata omits it from the model.
